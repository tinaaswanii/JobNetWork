🔑3 formulas that open every door

Almost every question fits one of these. Learn the three, and you can answer questions you have never seen.

Present, Past, Futurefor "Tell me about yourself" and "Why this role?"
STARfor every "Tell me about a time..." question
Claim, Proof, Valuefor strengths and "Why should we hire you?"

⚠️ Never memorize a sample word for word. Keep the structure, use your own real story.

👋Tell me about yourself

Not your life story. A 45 to 60 second trailer of you.

Present: who you are now→Past: 1 or 2 proofs→Future: why this role

⚠️ Do not read your resume line by line, and do not go past 90 seconds.

🎤 Sample answer

I am [Name], a final-year [degree] student at [college]. I enjoy [your main area]. During my [project or internship], I [one specific thing you did and the result], which taught me [skill]. I am now looking for a role like this one at [company], where I can apply [skill] and keep learning.

🪞What is your biggest weakness?

They test honesty. Pick a real, small weakness and show you are fixing it.

A real, small weakness→What you are doing about it→Proof of progress

⚠️ "I am a perfectionist" and "I work too hard" are the two most overused answers. Skip them.

🎤 Sample answer

I used to hesitate before speaking up in groups, because I wanted to be completely sure first. I have been working on it by [specific action, such as presenting in class]. Last [semester] I [small result], and it is getting easier.

💼Why should we hire you?

Your pitch. Pick the two skills the job description repeats most and prove each one.

Claim: I can do X→Proof: one real example→Value: what it gives them

⚠️ "I am hardworking and a quick learner" with no proof sounds like everyone else.

🎤 Sample answer

You need someone who can [skill 1] and [skill 2]. For [skill 1], I [proof]. For [skill 2], I [proof]. I also take feedback well, so I would be productive quickly and keep improving.

⭐Tell me about a time you faced a problem

STAR: spend most of your time on Action, because that is what they are judging.

Situation
Task
Action
Result

⚠️ Pick a real problem. Do not take credit for the whole team.

🎤 Sample answer

S: In my [project], [problem happened]. T: I was responsible for [your part]. A: I first [step 1], then [step 2], and found the cause was [cause]. I fixed it by [fix] and added [check] so it would not repeat. R: [Outcome], and I learned to [lesson].

🙋Do you have any questions for us?

Always say yes. Your questions show how serious you are.

AskWhat does success look like in the first 6 months?
AskWhat does training for new joiners look like?
AskWhat is the team's biggest challenge right now?

⚠️ Do not say "No questions", and do not ask only about salary and leave in round one.
